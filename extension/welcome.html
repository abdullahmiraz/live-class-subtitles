RU → EN Live Subtitles

Live English subtitles for Russian audio in any tab. Free, no account, and the speech recognition runs on your own computer.

1Install the companion (once)

Chrome can't run speech recognition by itself, so a small free server runs on your PC. On Linux, paste this into a terminal:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdullahmiraz/ru-en-live-subtitles/main/install.sh | bash

It downloads the speech models once (~350 MB) and starts by itself at login. It uses ~40 MB of memory while idle; the models load only while subtitles are on. Manual setup and source: GitHub.

2Start subtitles

Open your class or video, then click the extension icon → Start, or press Alt+Shift+Y. Tip: pin the icon (puzzle piece → 📌).

3Make it yours

Basic: panel or YouTube-style subtitles, size, recording a class. Advanced: microphone, translator, auto-start sites. Extra: learning tools (word cards, vocabulary for Anki).

Privacy

Audio never leaves your computer: it goes only to the companion on your PC. Only the recognized text is sent to a free translator (Yandex or Google) to get the English. No accounts, no analytics. Privacy policy
