Live Class Subtitles

Live subtitles for Russian audio in any tab, in English or 37 other languages. Free, no account.

✓ You're ready. Nothing else to install. Speech recognition is built into Chrome, on Windows, Mac, Linux and ChromeOS.

1Start subtitles

Open your class or video, then click the extension icon → Start on this tab, or press Alt+Shift+Y. Tip: pin the icon (puzzle piece 🧩 → 📌).

2Make it yours

Basic: subtitle language, panel or YouTube-style subtitles, size, recording a class. Advanced: speech engine, microphone, translator, auto-start sites. Extra: learning tools (word cards, vocabulary for Anki). Hover any ⓘ for help.

3Optional: more privacy or more accuracy

On this computer (private)no install

Chrome downloads a Russian speech pack once; then the audio never leaves your computer. Popup → Advanced → Speech recognition → On this computer.

Companion app (best accuracy)free · Windows · Mac · Linux

Uses GigaAM, one of the most accurate open Russian speech models, on your own PC: better words, punctuation, and grammar details in word cards. Install it once:

Windows: download the installer, then double-click it.

ChromeOS: the companion app isn't available; the built-in speech recognition works fully.

It downloads the speech models once (~350 MB), starts by itself when you sign in and uses ~40 MB of memory while idle. Then choose popup → Advanced → Speech recognition → Companion app. Instructions for another system

Privacy

No accounts, analytics or tracking. With "Built into Chrome", Chrome sends the class audio to Google's speech service to turn it into text. "On this computer" and the companion app keep the audio on your PC. The recognized text is sent to a free translator (Yandex or Google) to get the translation. Privacy policy
